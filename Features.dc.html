Core Capabilities

Nine things every client uses

Reporting dashboards

Live views of sales, stock, cash, and headcount. Filter by branch, period, or product without exporting anything.

Approval workflows

Purchase orders, expenses, and leave routed to the right approver with limits you set, and a record of who signed off.

Role based access

Each role sees only its own data and screens. Salary figures stay with HR, margins stay with management.

Integrations

Connects to accounting packages, banking files, biometric attendance devices, and email or SMS gateways.

Mobile access

Approvals, leave requests, payslips, and stock lookups work from a phone browser, with no separate app to install.

Audit trails

Every edit records who changed what and when, so month end reconciliation stops being a search exercise.

Document templates

Invoices, quotations, payslips, and letters carry your branding and wording, editable without a developer.

Data migration

We bring your existing masters, balances, and history across, and reconcile them against your old records before go live.

Backups and recovery

Scheduled backups with a tested restore procedure, on your servers or hosted, whichever you prefer.
