Partner Tiers

Two ways to work with us

Start with a referral. Move to reseller once you know how we work.

Referral partner

Introduce a client. We handle the scoping call, proposal, and delivery. You get paid when the deal closes.

  • No delivery work on your side
  • Commission on signed contract value
  • You are kept in the loop throughout

Reseller partner

Higher margin

You own the client relationship and contract. We deliver the implementation behind the scenes, under your brand if you prefer.

  • Everything in Referral
  • Wholesale delivery pricing
  • White label option on request
  • Dedicated partner contact
