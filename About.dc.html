Company Overview

What we do, in plain terms

Capricorn Consultancy helps businesses transform their processes with practical technology and clear guidance. We start with how the work moves through your organisation, identify where information gets lost, and then deliver the systems that close those gaps.

Our work spans three areas: enterprise resource planning, human resource management, and custom software development. Because the same team advises and builds, nothing is lost between the recommendation and the result.

We work with small and mid sized businesses across manufacturing, distribution, and services, and we stay involved after go live.

Mission and vision

Our Mission

To give growing businesses the systems and clarity of a large organisation, without the cost or complexity that usually comes with them. Every engagement should leave the client with a process they understand and software they can run themselves.

Our Vision

To be the long term technology partner our clients call first, known for honest advice, dependable delivery, and software that keeps earning its place years after it is installed.

How We Work

Four steps, whatever the size of the job

The same sequence applies to a two week review and a six month build. Only the depth changes.

STEP 01

Understand

We map current processes with the people who run them, not with the org chart.

STEP 02

Recommend

A written plan with scope, cost, and the outcome you should expect from it.

STEP 03

Deliver

Configuration or development, with training and a parallel run built in.

STEP 04

Support

Ongoing help and adjustments, from someone who knows your configuration.
